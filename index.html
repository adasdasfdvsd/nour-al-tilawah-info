Nour Al Tilawah Quran Publisher

A personal automation tool used by the operator of the Nour Al Tilawah YouTube channel to prepare Quran recitation videos with synchronized Arabic text, English translation and nature footage.

What the tool does

The tool uploads videos and thumbnails, sets titles and descriptions, schedules publication, and checks the publication status of the operator’s own videos. It uses the YouTube Data API with the channel owner’s authorization.

Why Google access is requested

Authorization lets the tool verify the destination channel, upload approved content, and confirm its scheduled or public status. It is used only for the operator’s channel; this is not a public service that connects other people’s accounts.

Publication and notifications

Videos are scheduled for Sunday at 09:00 UTC, Tuesday at 14:00 UTC and Friday at 12:00 UTC. Upload links, publication confirmations and error alerts are sent to the operator’s configured Telegram chat.

Read the privacy policy for how authorization and video metadata are handled, and the terms of use for the scope of this personal tool.
